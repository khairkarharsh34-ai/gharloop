FAQ

Questions people ask us

Do residents need to download anything?

Not right now. Open app.gharloop.com on any phone and, on iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens like a normal app. Play Store and App Store versions are coming.

Can residents log in with their mobile number?

Yes. Residents can sign up with a mobile number or an email and a password. The Society Admin approves every new member before they can see society information.

Who can see my phone number?

Only you. Neighbours see your name and flat, not your number. Numbers in the services list are shown only with the person's consent.

How does SOS work?

You choose the type of emergency and confirm. The guard on duty and your committee get a red alert with your name and flat right away. You also get one-tap buttons to call Police (100), Ambulance (108), Fire (101), 112 and the Women helpline (1091).

Does Ghar Loop collect maintenance payments?

Not yet. Today it records dues and payments and shows the society's spending. Online payment is on our roadmap.

How long does setup take?

Usually a day. We create your society, the committee invites residents, and the admin approves them as they join.
